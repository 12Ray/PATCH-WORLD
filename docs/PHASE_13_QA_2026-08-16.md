# Phase 13 QA — 2026-08-16

This record covers the pre-commit Phase 13 candidate in the local working tree.
It is runtime and automated evidence, not a substitute for the Phase 10.5 human
three-weapon balance sample.

## Automated gates

- `flutter analyze`: pass, zero findings.
- `flutter test`: 288/288 pass.
- Asset contracts: three survival enemies and four bosses each expose eight
  distinct 256×256 RGBA frames with transparent corners and stable display
  dimensions.
- Locale/aspect matrix: Korean, English, and Japanese critical UI passes at
  1280×720 (16:9), 1280×800 (16:10), and 2560×1080 (21:9).
- Input regression: keyboard J/K/L, movement, jump, parry, arrow keys, touch
  movement, touch actions, and sword double-tap dash pass.
- Entity budgets: active survival enemies stay inside the stage cap, enemy
  projectiles stay below 40, and transient combat effects stay below 96 and
  release their slots after expiry.
- Player presentation: combat one-shots keep a fixed component size and pivot,
  do not inherit idle bob/rotation, and return to identity after completion.
- Survival Crawler contact no longer deals immediate damage. A 0.46-second
  contraction ring precedes a range-checked bite, and moving out before the
  resolve frame avoids the hit.

## Open-field revision after visual playtest feedback

- The Survival Nexus expanded from 1,920×1,080 to 2,880×1,620, nine times the
  logical viewport area.
- Ten internal solid obstacles, four central phase walls, and three permanent
  damage zones were removed. Only the outer world boundary and temporary boss
  encounter seal remain solid.
- The previous generated background was removed from the Flutter asset tree
  and retained under `docs/visual-concepts/rejected/` for design history.
- Runtime now draws a quiet code-native gradient, low-contrast 120-pixel grid,
  subtle region bounds, and a central orientation ring.
- Web release visual QA confirmed the open field, readable combat silhouettes,
  zero browser console errors, and no request for the retired background asset.

## Enemy-density revision after open-field playtest feedback

- Opening pressure now starts with six Crawlers and one Rift Stalker instead of
  two Crawlers and one anomaly.
- The first reinforcement moved from 4.0 to 1.6 seconds, while the Boot-stage
  wave interval changed from 4.4–3.4 to 3.0–2.4 seconds.
- Normal reinforcements now enter 540–720 pixels from the player instead of at
  the far edge of the 2,880×1,620 world.
- Threat budgets increased at all four difficulty stages, while the existing
  24/32/40 staged active-enemy caps remain unchanged for performance safety.
- Static analysis and density-focused director, balance, and arena integration
  tests pass. Browser visual recheck was blocked by the local-preview browser
  URL policy after a transient empty response; no alternate browser bypass was
  used.

## J/K/L survival choice shortcuts

- Survival patch, weapon-upgrade, and item-reward three-choice overlays now map
  J/K/L to the left, middle, and right card respectively.
- Every card displays its shortcut badge, including compact stacked layouts.
- Choice input is intercepted before combat input and cleared after selection,
  so J/K/L cannot leak into attack, special, or interaction queues.
- The ordinary gameplay bindings remain unchanged when no choice is pending.
